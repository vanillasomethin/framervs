01 / Place & context

Begin with the setting.

Kerala's varied landscapes, rainfall patterns, vegetation and settlement forms call for site-specific design decisions. The character of a project is shaped by local conditions, client priorities, construction methods and long-term upkeep.

Kerala project enquiries, considered on a project-by-project basis. Our ability to take on a site depends on location, scope and coordination requirements.

Architectural design

Interior design
