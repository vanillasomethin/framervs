04 / Budget planning

Use cost guides as a starting point, not a quotation.

Our house construction cost guide for Karnataka and interior design cost guide explain the main cost components and planning considerations. They are regional reference material, not a fixed rate or a Kasaragod-specific estimate. Labour, materials, site conditions, specification and project scope can change the final budget.

The project cost estimator can help organise an initial brief. A realistic project estimate should be prepared only after the location, scope, specifications and local rates have been reviewed.

05 / Prepare your brief

A few details help us understand the project.

When you enquire, include the Kasaragod locality, whether the site is a plot or an existing building, the approximate plot or built-up area, the type of space, your priorities and your intended timeline. A site plan, photographs or a simple list of rooms can help clarify the first conversation.

If you have a budget range, share it as a planning guide. The scope of design services, site visits and any travel requirements can then be discussed transparently before proceeding.
