01 / The studio

Design shaped by people, place and purpose.

Based in Mangalore (Mangaluru), Vanilla & Somethin' works across architecture and interior design, with related capabilities in landscape concepts, BIM and visualisation. We begin by understanding the site, the people who will use the space, the project priorities and the level of involvement required.

For projects in Mangaluru and Dakshina Kannada, the coastal setting is part of the design brief. Orientation, daylight, rainfall, humidity, material performance, maintenance and the character of the neighbourhood all deserve consideration early in the process.

We discuss each enquiry individually. Project location, scope, programme and site-coordination requirements help establish whether the studio is the right fit.

05 / Common questions

A few useful things to know before getting in touch.

Do you take up residential architecture projects in Mangalore?

We consider residential and selected commercial enquiries in Mangaluru and the wider Dakshina Kannada region. Fit depends on the brief, site, scope, programme and the level of coordination required.

Can I approach you only for interior design?

Yes. Interior design enquiries can be discussed independently, including space planning, materials, furniture and joinery, and fit-out documentation.

Can you provide an exact project cost before a site discussion?

An exact figure needs a defined scope and project information. The online estimator and cost guides are intended for early planning only; a project-specific estimate follows a clearer brief and review.

Do you work outside Mangalore?

We are based in Mangalore and review suitable enquiries from other locations individually. Travel, site involvement and remote coordination are agreed according to the project.

06 / Start a conversation

Tell us what you are planning.

Share the project location, type of space, approximate area, intended timeline and the stage you are at. That gives us a useful starting point for discussing the next step.
