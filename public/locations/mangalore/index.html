01 / Place & context

Begin with the setting.

Mangalore and the wider Dakshina Kannada region bring together coastal climate, varied neighbourhoods and a mix of traditional and contemporary ways of living. A successful design response begins with the specific site, its orientation, local conditions and the needs of the people who will use the space.
