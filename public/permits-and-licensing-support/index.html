02 / Kerala and K-SMART

Building permissions and trade licence context.

Kerala's K-SMART platform includes building permission and business facilitation services such as trade and industry licences. The Local Self Government Department also publishes guidance on building permit workflows and rule-compliance tools.

We can help prepare architectural drawings and project information within our appointed scope and coordinate with the registered architect, engineer or other licensee responsible for the application. Trade licensing, statutory declarations, fees, NOCs and final approvals are determined by the relevant local body and competent authorities.

03 / Karnataka

Local authority requirements vary by jurisdiction.

Building plan approval and trade licence procedures depend on the relevant municipal corporation, municipality, town panchayat or gram panchayat. Greater Bengaluru has its own building plan approval system. For tourism projects, homestay registration and other hospitality approvals are handled through the applicable state tourism and local authority frameworks.

We can help develop design information and coordinate with the appointed local professionals. We do not represent that one portal or approval route applies across all of Karnataka.

04 / Saudi Arabia

Design coordination for projects in the Kingdom.

For projects in Saudi Arabia, building permits and commercial licensing are processed through the relevant competent platforms and authorities, including Balady and Civil Defense where applicable. We can discuss design documentation and coordination with the locally authorised engineering office or specialist appointed to the project. Official submissions and certifications must be completed by the authorised parties.

05 / Project-specific coordination

Clinics, cafes, restaurants and other regulated spaces.

Different business activities can trigger different requirements. A clinic may require healthcare-specific planning and specialist review. A cafe or restaurant may need food-service layouts, kitchen coordination, fire and life-safety input, accessibility, signage and municipal licensing information. The precise checklist depends on the activity, location, building and authority.

We can develop the spatial design and coordinate the relevant specialist inputs. We do not issue licences, certify compliance on behalf of authorities or guarantee approvals.
