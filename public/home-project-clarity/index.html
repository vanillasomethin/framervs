A few useful clarifications

What this does — and doesn't — mean.

Can you guarantee the final construction cost?

No. Early cost planning is indicative and depends on scope, specifications, site conditions, market rates, contractor pricing and decisions. Detailed estimates require defined scope and appropriate specialist inputs.

Does project oversight mean full-time site supervision?

Not automatically. Site visits, reporting, coordination and supervision are different services. Frequency, responsibilities and exclusions must be stated in the written appointment.

Can I approach you with an existing plan or contractor quotation?

Yes. Share the material and questions you want answered. The studio will confirm what can be reviewed within an agreed advisory scope.

Where is the studio based?

Vanilla & Somethin' is based in Mangalore. Enquiries from other locations are considered based on project fit and practical meeting and site-visit requirements.
