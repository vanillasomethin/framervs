How much does interior design cost in Karnataka?

Interior costs depend on what is being designed and supplied: modular kitchen, wardrobes, fixed furniture, ceilings, lighting, flooring, wall finishes, loose furniture, appliances and styling. A full-home interior rate per square foot can hide major differences in scope, and the area basis matters.

A Mangaluru market-rate tracker published in October 2026 reports the following indicative bands for full-home interiors, calculated per square foot of carpet area. These are third-party market estimates, not a quotation or price list from Vanilla & Somethin'.

What changes the price of a home interior?

Extent of work

One kitchen and two wardrobes are very different from a complete home with ceilings, lighting, wall panelling, furniture and styling.

Materials and hardware

Laminate, acrylic, veneer, PU, solid wood, plywood grade, moisture resistance, drawer systems, hinges and handles all change cost.

Design complexity

Curves, integrated lighting, concealed doors, custom profiles, detailed joinery and non-standard dimensions add design and fabrication time.

Site and coordination

Existing conditions, wall tolerances, electrical/plumbing changes, lift access, floor level and installation logistics affect final cost.

2BHK and 3BHK interior budgets: how to estimate

Start with the rooms and components rather than choosing a package by bedroom count alone. Record kitchen length and configuration, wardrobe shutter area, number of beds, storage, TV units, ceiling coverage, lighting, curtains and appliances. A 2BHK with only kitchen and wardrobes can cost much less than a fully furnished 2BHK with extensive ceiling and wall work.

  1. Measure the carpet area and list every room.
  2. Decide which items are essential now and which can be phased.
  3. Prepare a finish and hardware schedule for each fixed-furniture item.
  4. Keep civil modifications, electrical changes and plumbing changes separate from joinery.
  5. Compare vendor quotes against the same drawings, material specifications and quantities.

Interior design for homes in Mangalore and Kasaragod

Coastal homes need material choices that respond to humidity, ventilation, daylight, cleaning and maintenance. A finish that performs well in a dry interior may not be the best choice near the coast. Kitchen and bathroom detailing, substrate preparation, edge sealing, hardware and ventilation should be considered together.

Frequently asked questions

What is the interior design cost per sq ft in Mangalore?

A published Mangaluru market tracker lists indicative full-home interior bands of about ₹848–₹1,484/sq ft carpet for basic, ₹1,484–₹2,650 for mid-range and ₹2,650–₹4,770 for premium. These are market estimates, not fixed quotations.

How much does a 2BHK interior cost?

It depends on carpet area and what is included. A kitchen and wardrobes-only scope is not comparable to a complete interior with beds, TV units, ceilings, lighting, curtains and loose furniture. Make a room-wise item list before comparing totals.

How much does a modular kitchen cost?

The Mangaluru market tracker reports laminate-finish kitchens at approximately ₹1,696–₹2,968 per sq ft of cabinet front and acrylic finishes at ₹2,438–₹4,240. Confirm whether the quote includes hardware, accessories, countertop, sink, appliances and installation.

Is interior design charged on carpet area or built-up area?

It depends on the pricing model. The full-home market bands cited here use carpet area, while kitchens and wardrobes use cabinet-front or shutter area. Always confirm the basis before multiplying a rate.

Does the interior cost include design fees?

Not always. Some vendors quote supply and installation only; design consultancy, drawings, procurement, project coordination and site visits may be separate. Check the agreement.
