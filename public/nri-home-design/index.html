01 / Designed around distance

Being abroad shouldn't make the design process unclear.

When you are planning a home from another country, the first challenge is turning conversations, references and family requirements into a design brief that everyone understands. We can begin with an online consultation, review the information you already have and identify what needs to be measured, surveyed or confirmed before design progresses.

Our studio is based in Mangalore. Project location, site access, travel, consultant coordination and the extent of on-site involvement must be agreed in writing for each appointment. We do not imply that every project can be supervised remotely or that construction execution is automatically included in a design commission.

02 / A clear process

From first conversation to design decisions.

01 · Online briefing

Discuss your family, lifestyle, site, aspirations, approximate area, budget priorities and timeline over a video call.

02 · Site information

Review available plot documents, survey, photographs, existing drawings and local constraints. Identify any additional site information required.

03 · Concept and planning

Develop the agreed architectural or interior design scope and present drawings and visual material for discussion.

04 · Reviews and approvals

Record feedback, design decisions, revisions and approvals at agreed milestones so the brief remains clear across time zones.

05 · Technical documentation

Prepare the drawing and specification package included in the appointment. Consultant and statutory submissions are defined separately where applicable.

06 · Site-stage support

Site visits, contractor coordination, progress reviews and execution support are offered only where included in the agreed scope.

03 / Who we work with

For homeowners and families based overseas.

Whether you are based in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, the UK, Europe, the US, Canada or Australia, the first step is the same: tell us where the property is and what you are planning. We can discuss meeting times that work across time zones.

This service is intended for non-resident Indians and overseas families with a project in our practical service area. Availability, travel and site involvement are confirmed case by case.

05 / Prepare for your first call

What to share with us.

If available, send the property location, plot dimensions or survey, photographs, existing floor plans, approximate built-up area, room requirements, budget range, preferred timeline and any references that describe the kind of home you have in mind. You do not need to have every answer before the first conversation.

06 / Common questions
Do I need to travel to India to start the design?

No. The initial briefing can be online. Any later site visit, survey or in-person meeting will be planned according to the project and agreed scope.

Can you manage construction while I am abroad?

Construction management and site supervision are not automatic parts of a design appointment. We will clarify what site-stage services are available, who is responsible for execution and how often reporting or visits can be provided before accepting the project.

Can my family member coordinate locally?

Yes, a nominated family contact can be included in project communication where you authorise it. Design decisions and approvals should remain documented with the appointed client.

Can you work with our existing contractor?

We can discuss coordination with your contractor. The responsibilities, drawing package, response times and any site reviews need to be defined in the appointment.

What should I do first?

Send us the site location and a short description of your plans through the contact page. We can arrange an initial discussion and identify the next useful step.
