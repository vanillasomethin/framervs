01 / AI-integrated workflows

AI as a supporting tool, with architects in control.

We integrate AI-integrated tools into selected stages of research, information organisation, design exploration, documentation support and reporting. These tools help the team process information and explore options more efficiently. They do not replace architectural judgement, technical verification, consultant advice or client approval.

Information and research

Organising project briefs, reference material, meeting notes, requirements and design information for easier retrieval and review.

Design exploration

Supporting early option exploration, visual references and structured comparison while keeping final design decisions with the design team.

Documentation support

Helping structure action lists, review notes, issue registers and project updates for team checking and approval.

Reporting support

Preparing clearer summaries of milestones, open items, decisions and project status from information maintained by the team.

02 / In-house dashboards

One organised view of progress and budgets.

Our in-house dashboards bring key project information into a structured view. Depending on the appointment and project setup, this can include:

Milestones and progress

Planned stages, target dates, current status, completed activities and upcoming deliverables.

Tasks and responsibilities

Action items, assigned owners, due dates, dependencies and items awaiting input.

Budget visibility

Approved budget allocations, estimate revisions, recorded commitments, change items and cost updates.

Reviews and decisions

Design submissions, client comments, approvals, revision records and important project decisions.

Dashboard information reflects the latest updates recorded by the project team. It is not an independent site sensor or automatic verification of construction progress. Reporting frequency, access and included modules are agreed for each project.

03 / A more accountable process

Useful information, clear ownership and human review.

Digital tools are most useful when responsibilities are clear. We define who updates project information, who reviews it and how decisions are recorded. Budgets and progress are discussed against the agreed scope, programme and available project information.

Construction supervision, contractor management, quantity surveying, cost certification and continuous site monitoring are separate services unless explicitly included in the appointment.
