What the consultation is not

The consultation is a free first-step discussion followed by a short email recap. It is deliberately narrower than a design or construction engagement. It is not:

  • a cost estimate or bill of quantities;
  • a quotation from a contractor or supplier;
  • structural, foundation or soil advice;
  • an approval or permit assessment;
  • contractor supervision.

Each of those needs the right specialist and a defined engagement. If a question sits outside the consultation, we will say so and point you to who is best placed to answer it.

How it works

  1. Share your brief through the enquiry form, including property type, location and the stage your project has reached.
  2. We confirm a time and hold the consultation online or in person in Mangalore. There is no charge.
  3. The consultation covers your requirements, the site and the budget conversation. Bring any drawings or quotations and we will point out the questions worth asking.
  4. You receive a short email recap of the next steps we discussed.
  5. If you want to proceed, we provide a detailed proposal with deliverables, responsibilities, fees and timeline.

Nothing is assumed beyond the consultation.

Why start here

Homeowners rarely lack options. They have too many: designers, builders, contractors, online calculators and opinions from relatives. What most are missing is a clear sequence for making decisions, and someone whose job is to look out for their interests from the start.

Unclear scope is where budgets and relationships usually break down. A roof that was never specified, a kitchen assumed to be included, or a site condition nobody raised can each change the final number. Our guide to architect, engineer and contractor roles explains the usual split of responsibilities, and our note on comparing construction quotations shows how to read two proposals side by side.

A documented starting point protects your decisions later. When you do appoint an architect, engineer or contractor, you have a written record of what you wanted, what was known about the site and what was still open.

Frequently asked questions

Is the consultation free?

Yes. The Home Project Clarity Consultation is free. It is a first conversation to understand your goals, site, project stage and budget range, and to agree sensible next steps. Detailed work such as drawing or quotation reviews, site surveys and design is set out in a written proposal if you choose to continue.

Do I have to hire you afterwards?

No. There is no obligation. You can use the next steps we agree with any architect, contractor or specialist, whether or not you continue with us.

Can NRI families do this remotely?

Yes. The consultation can be held online, which suits families living abroad or outside Mangalore. Share site documents and photos before the session so the time is spent on decisions, not on gathering information. Families planning from overseas can also read our NRI home design page.

What should I bring?

Bring whatever you already have: site documents, rough sketches, photos of the plot or existing house, any quotations or drawings, and a budget range. A rough range is enough to start.

Can you review a quotation or plan I already have?

We can look at it with you during the consultation and point out obvious gaps and the questions to put to the contractor or designer. A detailed scope review of drawings or quotations is separate work that we quote in writing first. Structural or legal checks need the relevant specialist.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Mangalore. Enquiries from Dakshina Kannada, Udupi and Kasaragod are assessed by scope, travel and site coordination. For our local base and how we work in the city, see our Mangalore page.
