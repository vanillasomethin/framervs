Different businesses. Different spatial needs.

Cafes & restaurants

Kiosks & compact retail

Retail stores & supermarkets

Commercial buildings

Building planning and architectural design for business premises, mixed-use environments and customer-facing facilities.

02 / Design scope

From the first layout to the details people experience.

Depending on the brief, our scope can include architectural concept design, space planning, interior design, material and finish selection, furniture and joinery design, lighting coordination, signage integration, 3D visualisation, design development and construction documentation.

For hospitality and retail projects, we consider the relationship between customer areas, staff movement, service functions, storage and operational requirements. Specialist kitchen, MEP, fire and life-safety, structural, statutory and other consultant services are identified and coordinated as required; they are not assumed to be included unless expressly agreed.

03 / Designed for operators and brands

A commercial interior has to work as well as it looks.

A strong commercial environment should support the way a business actually operates. We begin by understanding the brand, target users, site constraints, operating model, capacity, equipment needs, budget priorities and delivery timeline. These inform the layout and design decisions rather than treating the interior as decoration alone.

For multi-location brands or repeatable formats, we can discuss design guidelines, standard details and adaptable spatial components suited to the agreed rollout scope.

06 / Common questions
Do you design small kiosks as well as full commercial buildings?

Yes. We welcome enquiries ranging from compact kiosks and small retail fit-outs to larger commercial building and interior design briefs. The scope is assessed against the site, requirements and project resources.

Can you design a cafe or restaurant from scratch?

We can discuss architectural and interior design for cafes and restaurants, including planning, customer and staff circulation, seating, finishes and coordination with specialist consultants. Kitchen equipment and technical consultant scope must be defined separately.

Can you work with an existing brand identity?

Yes. Share your brand guidelines, operational requirements and references so the spatial design can be developed in alignment with the existing identity.

Do you handle execution and site supervision?

Site visits, supervision, contractor coordination and execution-related services depend on location and the agreed appointment. These are not automatically included in a design scope.
