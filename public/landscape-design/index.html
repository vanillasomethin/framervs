Landscape architecture · Gardens · Outdoor spaces

Outdoor spaces shaped by place, climate and everyday life.

Vanilla & Somethin' develops landscape concepts and outdoor environments that connect buildings with their setting. From private gardens and courtyards to terraces, rooftop spaces and commercial landscapes, each design responds to the site and the people who use it.

01 / Spaces we shape

Landscape design across different scales.

Private gardens

Garden layouts, planting character, shaded sitting areas, paths and transitions between indoor and outdoor living.

Courtyards and arrival spaces

Entry sequences, internal courtyards, thresholds, paving, planting and visual connections to the building.

Terraces and rooftop gardens

Outdoor rooms, planters, seating, shade, surface choices and planting strategies suited to exposed conditions.

Commercial outdoor spaces

Landscape concepts for cafes, restaurants, offices, retail environments and shared customer spaces.

Residential site planning

Building-to-landscape relationships, outdoor circulation, open spaces, boundary treatments and site-responsive design.

Community and shared landscapes

Shared gardens, pedestrian routes, gathering spaces and landscape character for multi-user environments.

02 / Our design approach

Start with the site, not a template.

We consider orientation, sun exposure, rainfall, drainage conditions, soil, existing vegetation, privacy, views and maintenance expectations. In coastal Karnataka and Kerala, the monsoon, humidity and local planting context can strongly influence material and planting decisions.

Depending on the brief, the design scope may include site analysis, concept layouts, circulation, hardscape, planting palettes, outdoor lighting concepts, furniture and feature elements, irrigation coordination, 3D visualisation and design documentation.

03 / Connected to architecture

Landscape and building design considered together.

Outdoor spaces are planned as part of the wider architectural experience. We coordinate landscape ideas with entrances, windows, views, levels, access, drainage intent and the use of adjacent indoor spaces. Specialist engineering, detailed irrigation design, arboricultural advice and statutory submissions can be brought into the scope where required.
