House construction cost in Karnataka: a practical 2026 guide

Planning a home in Karnataka? Understand construction cost per sq ft, compare Mangaluru market ranges and see what a 1,000, 1,500 or 2,000 sq ft house budget may look like before you speak to contractors.

How much does it cost to build a house in Karnataka?

There is no single construction rate for Karnataka. City, site access, soil and foundation conditions, structural system, number of floors, specification, labour availability and what the contractor includes can change the final budget substantially. A rate quoted per square foot is useful for early planning only when the measurement basis and inclusions are stated.

Published Mangaluru market references currently show a wide spread: one local contractor lists packages from ₹1,750/sq ft for basic, ₹2,000/sq ft for standard and ₹2,300/sq ft for premium; another local cost guide gives a broader ₹1,500–₹3,500/sq ft range. A separate market tracker reports a standard band of approximately ₹2,045–₹2,649/sq ft. These are third-party published indicators, not an official Karnataka rate or a quote from our studio.

Practical reading: for an early Mangaluru feasibility check, compare scenarios around ₹1,750, ₹2,000, ₹2,500 and ₹3,500 per sq ft, then replace those assumptions with a measured design, specification and local contractor quotations.

What is included — and what often gets missed?

Usually in a base construction package

RCC and masonry work, basic plastering, flooring and standard finish items may be included, depending on the contractor's written scope.

Often excluded or separately priced

Land, architect and consultant fees, approvals, compound walls, gates, site development, retaining walls, borewell, septic/STP, utility connections, premium joinery, loose furniture, appliances and landscaping.

Site-specific cost drivers

Rock or weak soil, slope, high water table, difficult access, coastal exposure, drainage, retaining structures and monsoon sequencing can materially change cost.

Design and specification decisions

Span and cantilevers, floor-to-floor heights, façade systems, window sizes, roof form, tile formats, sanitary fittings and bespoke carpentry influence the budget.

How to compare construction rates fairly

  1. Confirm whether the quote uses built-up area, plinth area or carpet area.
  2. Ask for a room-by-room and trade-wise scope, not just one headline rate.
  3. Compare structural drawings, foundation assumptions, steel and concrete specifications.
  4. List doors, windows, flooring, sanitaryware, electrical points, waterproofing and paint brands/grades.
  5. Separate contractor overheads, taxes, professional fees and external development.
  6. Keep a contingency based on unresolved site and design decisions.

For Karnataka government works, the Public Works Department Schedule of Rates is a useful technical reference for item-wise rate analysis. It is not the same as a complete private residential turnkey package. The schedule itself notes that area-specific loading and quarterly material rates can apply.

Construction cost in Mangalore, Kasaragod and coastal Karnataka

Do not assume that one per-square-foot rate applies across the coast. Mangaluru, Udupi, Kasaragod and Kannur have different supplier networks, transport distances, local labour conditions, approval processes and site constraints. Coastal exposure, rainfall management, laterite/soil conditions, drainage and material availability should be assessed at the actual plot.

Frequently asked questions

What is the construction cost per sq ft in Karnataka in 2026?

There is no single official private-house rate. Published Mangaluru references range from about ₹1,750/sq ft for a basic package to ₹3,500/sq ft for premium work, with other market trackers showing different bands. Treat these as initial comparison points, not guaranteed prices.

How much does a 1,000 sq ft house cost in Mangalore?

At illustrative rates of ₹1,750–₹3,500/sq ft, the arithmetic range is ₹17.5–₹35 lakh before land and items excluded from the chosen construction scope. A real estimate needs the drawings, soil and foundation assumptions, specification and contractor inclusions.

Does construction cost per sq ft include interiors?

Not necessarily. A basic construction package may include only standard fixed finishes. Modular kitchen, wardrobes, loose furniture, appliances, curtains and bespoke joinery are often separate. Ask for a written inclusions schedule.

Is architect fee included in construction cost?

Often it is not. Architectural design, structural and MEP consultants, authority fees and site services should be identified as separate budget lines unless the contract explicitly includes them.

How much contingency should I keep?

Set a project-specific contingency after the scope and site risks are understood. Avoid using a generic percentage as a substitute for resolving soil, approvals, quantities, finish selections and exclusions.
