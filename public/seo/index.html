01 / The practice

Architecture and interiors, considered as one.

Our work moves between architectural design, interior environments, landscape, digital modelling and visual communication. Each brief begins with understanding the people who will use a space, the character of its setting and the practical conditions that shape it.

From early concepts and spatial planning to design development, construction documentation and visualisation, we aim to make the design intent clear at every stage.

02 / Services

What we can help design.

Architectural design

Concept development, schematic design, design development, construction documentation, passive design and 3D visualisation.

Interior design

Space planning, materials and finishes, furniture and joinery, fit-out drawings, styling and staging.

Landscape design

Site-responsive landscape concepts, hardscape, planting, lighting and irrigation planning.

BIM & digital design

BIM modelling, coordination, clash detection, as-built models and parametric workflows.

Visualisation

3D modelling, architectural renders, walkthroughs, animations and design communication.

Project support

Consultant coordination, design supervision, site reviews and documentation support.

05 / Frequently asked

A few things to know before starting.

What types of projects do you take on?

Our listed services include architectural design, interior design, landscape design, BIM and digital design, visualisation and project support. Share your brief so we can assess scope and fit.

Can you work on a project outside Mangalore?

We are based in Mangalore and invite enquiries from other locations. Travel, site involvement and remote coordination depend on the project scope and will be agreed before appointment.

Do you provide both architecture and interior design?

Yes. Architectural and interior design are both part of our studio's listed services. The exact scope can be tailored to the project.

How do I discuss a project?

Use the contact page to share your location, project type, approximate requirements and preferred way to connect. We will review the brief and discuss next steps.
