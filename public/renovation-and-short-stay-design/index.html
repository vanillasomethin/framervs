04 / Registration and use

Design and approvals need to be considered together.

An Airbnb listing is not itself a planning permission or operating licence. Whether a property can operate as a homestay, serviced villa, holiday home or other tourist accommodation depends on the property's location, ownership, use and current rules. We can help identify design documentation and coordination needs, while applications and approvals remain with the relevant authorities and authorised professionals.
